The risk of false positives is considered low but will be monitored. | ChatGPT-4o was used to research typical accelerometer sampling rates and power consumption figures for modern smartphones. Aggregate alerts: The OBU groups BSMs by location and intent, triggering a single alert like "Pedestrian Group Ahead." 3. Require additional roadside infrastructure to confirm hidden pedestrians. | 1. Early warning value 2. Practical feasibility 3. Cost and infrastructure needs 4.
